About

Grid It is a graphic design grid generator inspired by the Swiss typographic tradition. Define paper size, margins, columns and rows — then export a clean SVG for use in any design tool.

Classic presets reference the work of Josef Müller-Brockmann, Jan Tschichold and the Van de Graaf canon.

Press O to export SVG.
